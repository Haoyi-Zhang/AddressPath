# AI use and accountable authorship record

Generative AI materially assisted research planning, formalization, code generation, synthetic-model construction, testing, analysis, literature organization, typesetting, and prose. This record is intentionally broader than a “language editing only” disclosure.

Before any external submission, accountable human authors must independently inspect the mathematical statements and proofs, execute and review the artifact, verify every source and architecture mapping, decide which claims they endorse, make substantive intellectual contributions, satisfy the target venue's current authorship policy, and accept responsibility for the complete work. An AI system is not an author and cannot assume responsibility, consent to publication, manage conflicts, or answer post-publication questions.
